METHOD - Part 1 (Real Data: techi_articles.csv)

Collection

On 3 Oct 2026, I opened the six section pages under techi.com/category/: AI, Markets, Crypto, Breakthroughs, Policy, and Guides.

The pages were public and did not require a login. I loaded them one at a time, with about 2 seconds between page loads.

From each section page, I took the first 20 articles in the order shown, giving 120 rows in total.

url, headline, author, and date_shown were copied exactly as displayed. `section` is the section page where the article was found.

Coding rules

headline_word_count: Headline split on spaces.

contains_a_number: 1 if the headline contains any digit. Years count; written numbers do not.

names_a_company: 1 if the headline names a company, product, or stock ticker. Agencies such as FTC and NSA, coins such as Ether and Zcash, and open-source projects are coded as 0. Doubtful cases such as JAX, Worldcoin, Xinference, and Fenghe are also coded as 0.

format: Judged from the headline only:
- news = something new happened or shipped
- analysis = forecast, valuation, or argument
- explainer = background
- guide = how-to, checklist, or best-of
- review = product review

Checks

The CSV is UTF-8. The first save was Windows-1252.

Word counts and digit flags were recomputed in the Checks sheet of analysis.xlsx.

The checks show:
- 120 rows
- 0 blanks
- 0 word-count mismatches
- 0 digit-flag mismatches

Reproduce

The calculations can be checked in analysis.xlsx.

The same steps are available in analysis.py and analysis.ipynb. The two figures were generated from the Jupyter Notebook (analysis.ipynb).

Sample limits

- The 20 newest articles cover different time spans: 12 days for AI and 206 days for Guides.
- The oldest row is 11 Mar 2026, so this sample does not cover Nov 2025 or Feb 2026.
- Relative dates such as "5 days ago" were counted back from 3 Oct 2026, so they may be off by one day.
- Three articles appear in two sections, giving 120 rows but 117 unique URLs.
- I assumed the pages were ordered newest first. Markets contains one "1 day ago" row and then jumps to 19 Aug.
- "Dr Layloma Rashid" and "Layloma Rashid" appear to refer to the same person, but both names were kept exactly as shown.
- format and names_a_company are based on judgement rather than a measured field.
